Frequently Asked Questions

What tournament formats are supported?
At MVP, TourneyHub supports Single Elimination and Round Robin formats with head-to-head matches. We calculate automatic mathematically fair byes and provide deterministic scheduling. Group Stages, Swiss, and Double Elimination are on the roadmap.
How do you handle payments and entry fees?
To avoid payment gateway lock-in and heavy legal overhead, we provide a manual ledger. You can configure entry fees and prize pools, and the system automatically computes payouts and organizer fees. You record the actual movement of money (cash, Venmo, bank transfer) in the app to maintain an auditable ledger.
What disciplines can I run?
Anything with a head-to-head winner or score! Whether it's 1v1 Table Tennis, 2v2 Foosball, 5v5 League of Legends, or an 8-player Board Game squad. You configure the team size (1-10) and the result model (score-based or simple win/loss) per tournament.
How do I get started?
You can start creating tournaments immediately for free. While the platform is open-source and self-hostable, we provide everything you need to run your events right out of the box. Simply create a league and share the link with your players.
Is it really free?
Yes. The platform is fully open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license. You are free to run it yourself, modify it, and use it for your club or community without paying subscription fees.
